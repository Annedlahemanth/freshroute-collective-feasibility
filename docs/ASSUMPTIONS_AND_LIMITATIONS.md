# Assumptions and Limitations

FreshRoute is a secondary-research feasibility project. The decision model intentionally distinguishes between evidence and planning assumptions.

## Principal assumptions requiring validation

- contracted refrigerated 3PL route-day cost;
- customer delivery-price acceptance;
- number of route-compatible independent SMEs;
- typical order weight and payload mix;
- supplier and customer receiving windows;
- route stop times and actual Coventry routing;
- handling and coordination cost;
- start-up and insurance costs.

## Important limitations

- The Coventry FSA count is a screening universe, not an addressable-market estimate.
- No primary interview or survey evidence is presented as a project finding.
- Customer willingness to pay is not observed.
- The 21-SME ceiling is a derived capacity result under the current three-route configuration, not a demand forecast.
- The £275/day 3PL cost is a scenario, not a supplier quotation.
- The £27.89 and £29.76 price points are formula-derived thresholds, not accepted market prices.
- Food-waste reduction is treated as a potential pilot outcome, not a guaranteed commercial claim.

## Decision implication

The analysis supports controlled pre-pilot validation. It does not support immediate full-scale launch without replacing the main scenario inputs with commercial evidence.
