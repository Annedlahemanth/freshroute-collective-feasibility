# Artefact Guide

The Excel workbook is the primary Route 3 artefact. It is designed as a managerial decision-support model rather than a sales forecast.

## Start here

1. **Dashboard** — summary of the market, route, commercial and validation status.
2. **Artifact Guide** — workbook navigation and interpretation rules.
3. **Assumptions Register** — identifies which values are observed, published, scenario-based or derived.

## Main analysis sheets

### Route Feasibility
Tests supplier pickups, SME drops, distance, route time and payload assumptions. The current planning ceiling is 14 SME drops per route-day; the 16-drop scenario exceeds the 7.5-hour planning limit.

### 3PL Cost Envelope
Tests the relationship between contracted refrigerated route-day cost, break-even customer density and the delivery fee required for target operating margins.

### Customer Cost Comparator
Provides an illustrative comparison with the cost of owner/employee self-collection. It is a conversation aid only and is not evidence of willingness to pay.

### Forecast
Stress-tests cash exposure and start-up envelopes. It should not be read as a forecast of realised performance.

### Compliance Matrix
Allocates the principal registration, hygiene, temperature, traceability and custody controls across suppliers, FreshRoute, the contracted 3PL and SME recipients.

### Implementation / Risk Register
Translates the analysis into G0-G5 go/no-go gates and operational risk controls.

## Interpretation rule

A model output is only as strong as its inputs. Scenario values should be replaced with commercial or pilot evidence before a live operating decision is made.
